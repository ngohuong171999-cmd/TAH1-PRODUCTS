Teak Sun Lounger with Textilene Seat

Item No.: TH-001

Description

Outdoor sun lounger with a solid teak frame and a breathable textilene sling that dries quickly after rain. The backrest has multiple reclining positions, and small rear wheels make it easy to move around a garden or poolside.
